      Trainer Tips: Welcome to Competitive Pokémon GO!

      Our goal is to make competitive play fun, simple, and encouraging. Here are core rules to keep in mind:

      ✅ DO THIS

      • Save Your Stardust: Power up Pokémon only when you need them for an active team or cup.
      • Learn Fast Move Pacing: Count fast moves to predict when your opponent will fire a charged move!
      • Unlock 2nd Charged Moves: Having two charged move options gives you crucial type coverage.

      ❌ AVOID THIS

      • Don't Purify Mindlessly: Shadow Pokémon get a +20% Attack boost that is super powerful in PvP!
      • Don't Waste Elite TMs: Save Elite Fast/Charged TMs for exclusive event-only moves on high-tier mons.
      • Don't Get Discouraged: Everyone loses battles! Learning team matchups takes practice and patience.
